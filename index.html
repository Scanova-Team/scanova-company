Features

What Scanova does

AI scanning

Links, QR codes and emails go through AI models trained on datasets of known malicious links and email blacklists.

Secure sandbox

Open a link or file in a virtual environment that is separate from your device and your data, then end the session when you are done.

Detailed reports

Every scan ends with a security score and a breakdown of what was checked, so you can see why something was flagged.

Sco assistant

A chatbot that explains scan results, walks you through the app and gives practical advice on phishing and fraud.

How it works

What happens when you scan something

STEP 1

Scan or paste

Point the camera at a QR code, paste a link, or check an email by pasting it or uploading the .eml file.

STEP 2

Read the report

You get a security score and a breakdown: HTTPS, blacklist and redirects for links; sender domain, phishing keywords, embedded links and attachments for emails.

95Low risk 58Potential risk 23Possible risk
STEP 3

Open it in the sandbox

If you still need to look, the page opens in an isolated environment. Device, storage, downloads and scripts show their protection status, and the session auto-destroys when you close it.
